Core evidence

CAG Performance Audit of MGNREGS, Karnataka · Report No. 13 of 2025

The CAG found geo-tagged photos “captured from existing photograph”, a photo of a different shed, and the same photos reused across work stages, used to release payments.

CAG test-check findings from sampled gram panchayats, not state-wide totals. Not PRAMAAN results.

PRAMAAN is designed to make a field capture independently verifiable as evidence at the moment it is captured: a phone app that checks a 3-second clip on the device and issues a Certificate that a second party can check on a laptop Verifier Desk.

Everything shown in this deck is a presentation-only prototype or a proposed implementation for the event. Nothing has been built, run or measured yet.

Three checks · three verdict bands

No single check decides. Uncertainty is not fraud.

Motion Consistency is designed to ask whether the phone's own movement, from the gyroscope, matches the movement seen in the clip.

Geofence / Location Binding is designed to measure how far the capture is from the claimed registered site.

FFT-based Moiré / Recapture Detection is designed to look for the regular patterns that appear when a screen is filmed.

Each check will report PASS, FAIL, LOW_CONFIDENCE or UNAVAILABLE. An UNAVAILABLE check is left out; LOW_CONFIDENCE is uncertainty, not a sign of fraud.

Honest limitation

PRAMAAN proves capture authenticity, not scene truthfulness.

It cannot tell whether a structure built at the correct site meets the scheme's criteria.

Also stated openly

  • GPS spoofing with a mock-location app is a known gap; v2 mitigation: Wi-Fi / cell-tower cross-check.
  • Reuse of an earlier genuine capture of the same site is outside what a single capture check can see; camera-only capture raises the bar but does not detect it.
  • App-level signing, not hardware-attested: a rooted or modified device can bypass it; hardware attestation is roadmap only.

 

  • FFT-based Moiré / Recapture Detection will be tuned against specific screens at the venue, listed, with no claim of general validity.
  • Indoors, GPS can be slow or imprecise; poor GPS or a textureless scene is designed to give LOW_CONFIDENCE.
  • The event build will use a few hardcoded demo sites, not a site-registration system. PRAMAAN cannot force a capture to happen.

What makes us stand out · team

Offline, on-device trust tools for location already exist; at the Bengaluru City Battle, Team Nexus's Anchor was one. PRAMAAN targets a different object: the capture itself. In MGNREGS the geo-tagged photograph is the proof that releases public money, and the CAG's 2025 Karnataka audit found photos “captured from existing photograph”, a photo of a different shed, and the same photos reused across stages. A correct location alone does not show whether an image was captured live or recaptured from a screen. PRAMAAN is designed to fuse three checks, Motion Consistency, Geofence / Location Binding and FFT-based Moiré / Recapture Detection, into a certificate a second party can check independently. The checks, the certificate signing and the Verifier Desk will all be built live during the event, the phone app on the iQOO 15.
