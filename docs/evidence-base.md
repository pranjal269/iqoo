Use it as the hook ("the gate exists, and it's failing"), then move to E2 for the direct fit.

## E2 — CAG Performance Audit of MGNREGS, Karnataka (Report No. 13 of 2025) — VERIFIED ★ strongest fit

This is a Comptroller and Auditor General report laid before the Karnataka Legislature. It documents, in the auditor's own words, **exactly the attacks PRAMAAN targets**: recaptured, reused and fabricated geotagged photographs being used to release payments.

**Source:** https://cag.gov.in/webroot/uploads/download_audit_report/2025/English-final-pdf-MGNREGS-with-signature-069c27a99698a17.10858290.pdf (downloaded and text-searched 2026-09-26)

Verbatim findings, with page numbers from the report:

1. **Photo of a photo (recapture) → maps to the moiré/recapture check.** Appendix, Illustration-1, p. 116: *"Instead of geo-tagged onsite photograph, photograph captured from existing photograph was uploaded (18.07.2023) on Bhuvan portal to substantiate its completion under MGNREGS."* This related to ₹18,596 in wages for a house already built under a housing scheme.
2. **Old photos passed off as new site photos.** Para on work-splitting, p. 57: *"photographs in some of these cases uploaded on Bhuvan Portal were not genuine as these were not captured from the work sites but appeared to be taken from existing/old photographs."* This was in the context of 65 works split into 463, with ₹28.17 crore of irregular expenditure. **Note:** the ₹28.17 cr is the splitting irregularity, not a photo-fraud total. Don't conflate the two.
3. **Photo of a different structure → maps to the location-binding check.** p. 47–48 (Babalad (IK) GP, Kalaburagi): *"photograph of another existing waste management shed was uploaded … to show the execution of the work"*. Physical verification on 24.08.2024 found *"no structure /shed/ building was available at that location"*. Expenditure on the work was ₹18.45 lakh, and CAG calls it *"probable embezzlement of Government funds."*
4. **The same photos reused across stages.** p. 45–46: *"Payments in 12 cases were made on muster rolls generated though no work had been executed as seen from the Bhuvan portal and same photographs were uploaded for different stages of work."*
5. **The same house photographed for two different works.** Appendix, Illustration-2, p. 122: *"photographs of the same house were uploaded for both the works"*. Wages of ₹33,235 were credited to non-beneficiaries.

**How to use it honestly:** these are CAG *test-check* findings from sampled gram panchayats, not state-wide totals. Say "CAG found cases where…". Don't say "CAG found that X% of photos…".
